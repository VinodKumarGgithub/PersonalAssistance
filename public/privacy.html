Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 30, 2026 | App: Lara - Personal Assistance

Welcome to Lara - Personal Assistance. We value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data.

1. Purpose of the Application

Lara - Personal Assistance is an AI-powered personal assistant designed to help users interactively manage their Google Calendar events, schedule meetings, create Google Meet video links, and perform web searches.

2. Information We Collect & Use

When you authenticate using Google OAuth, we request read and write access to your Google Calendar scopes (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar).

  • Google Refresh Token: Encrypted securely at rest using AES-256-GCM encryption in our database and used solely to fetch, create, update, or delete calendar events upon your direct prompt.
  • Calendar Event Data: Event titles, descriptions, start/end times, location, and Google Meet video links are processed in-memory to display schedule details.

3. Google API Limited Use Disclosure

Lara - Personal Assistance's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

4. Data Storage & Security

We do not share, sell, or rent your personal information or Google user data to third parties. All access tokens are strictly encrypted at rest and used only to execute the calendar operations requested by you during session interaction.

5. Data Deletion & Revocation

You can disconnect your Google Calendar at any time by clicking the Disconnect option in the application interface, or by revoking access directly through your Google Account Permissions.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us via our project repository.
